When a stretch of text looks garbled, check for hands shifted one key to the right on a QWERTY keyboard before guessing words:
1. Isolate the garbled stretch, leaving already readable text alone.
2. Decode each character by moving one key left within its keyboard row: qwertyuiop[], asdfghjkl;', zxcvbnm,./. For example, o becomes i, l becomes k, [ becomes p, ; becomes l, and , becomes m. Keep spaces. Punctuation inside the garbled stretch may be a displaced letter; preserve actual sentence punctuation outside it.
3. Accept the decoded stretch when it forms coherent words in context. Preserve the decoded words in full rather than paraphrasing them or turning them into chat abbreviations. If a uniform shift does not produce coherent text, use ordinary typo correction instead.
Worked examples (typed -> decoded): "nolr tofr" -> "bike ride"; "[svl nsh" -> "pack bag". In the first, n->b, o->i, l->k, r->e; t->r, o->i, f->d, r->e. In the second, [->p, s->a, v->c, l->k; n->b, s->a, h->g.
